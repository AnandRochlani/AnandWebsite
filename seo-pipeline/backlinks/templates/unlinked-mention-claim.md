## How to personalize this

- `{{quote}}` is the exact sentence containing your name or the course name, copy-pasted. That's what makes this credible and instant to action.
- Keep it this short. This template has the highest reply rate of any of them precisely because the ask is trivial and you're saying thank you first.
- If the mention is on a page that scraped your content wholesale rather than mentioning you, this is the wrong template — ask for attribution or removal instead, and don't frame it as a favour.
- Never follow up more than once, and never argue. A mention without a link is still a win.
- Find these with quarterly searches for `"Anand Rochlani" -site:anandrochlani.com`, the course name in quotes, and distinctive sentences from your posts in quotes. Set up a Google Alert for the first two.
