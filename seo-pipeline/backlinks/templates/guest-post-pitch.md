## How to personalize this

- `{{their_article}}` must be a real article from **their** site that you actually read, named by title. Editors bin pitches that could have been sent to anyone.
- `{{gap}}` is the whole pitch: one sentence naming something their coverage is missing. "You've covered caching strategies but nothing on what breaks when the cache and the DB disagree" beats "I love your content".
- `{{article_title}}` should read like one of their existing headlines, not like one of yours.
- `{{credential}}` — one line, concrete: the Udemy course + student count, or the YouTube channel, or a merged PR to a well-known repo. One line. Do not list everything.
- Do NOT attach a draft unless their guidelines ask for one (freeCodeCamp does; InfoQ wants a proposal first).
- Do NOT mention wanting a backlink. The author bio link is standard; asking for it makes you look like an SEO buyer.
- Length check: if the email is longer than what's above, cut it.
