## How to personalize this

- Pitch the **episode**, not yourself. Bookers choose topics; guests come attached.
- `{{thesis}}` must be arguable — something a listener could disagree with. "System design interviews test a skill most engineers never use at work, and here's what to test instead" is a thesis. "An introduction to system design" is a syllabus.
- `{{episode_ref}}` — name a real episode. Podcasts get pitched constantly by people who've never listened.
- `{{credential}}` — one line. Course + students, or channel + subscribers. Not a bio.
- Don't ask for a link. Episode pages link guests by default.
- One follow-up after 7-10 days, then stop.
