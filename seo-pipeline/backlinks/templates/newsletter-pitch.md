## How to personalize this

- **Say it's yours.** Curators find out anyway, and the ones worth being in (Cooperpress, Better Dev Link) explicitly don't mind self-suggestions — they mind being deceived.
- `{{issue_ref}}` = one concrete thing from a recent issue. It's the only proof you actually read it.
- `{{one_liner}}` is how they'd write the blurb. Write it in their voice and you make their job easier, which is the whole game. 15-25 words.
- `{{why_their_readers}}` — one sentence tying the piece to their specific audience, not to developers in general.
- **One link per email, and not every week.** Submitting every new post turns you into a filter rule.
- If the newsletter has a submission form, use the form. Don't email around it.
- For podcasts use `podcast-pitch.md` instead — the ask is different.
