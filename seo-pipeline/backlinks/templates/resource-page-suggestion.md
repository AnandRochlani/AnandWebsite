## How to personalize this

- `{{existing_resource}}` proves you opened the page. Name something actually on it.
- `{{what_it_covers}}` should say what a student *gets* in one sentence — "walks through the whole URL-shortener design with the capacity maths written out" — not "is a comprehensive guide".
- `{{free_offer}}` is the part that actually works with student chapters and career centres: offer a free 45-minute session or Q&A for their members. Drop this line for curated GitHub lists and public resource pages where it doesn't apply.
- **Never** mention the paid Udemy course in this email. A page owner who thinks they're being used as a funnel says no. The course sells itself once people read the free posts.
- Never offer money, a "partnership fee", or a reciprocal link deal for a .edu link. That is a link scheme, and it gets both sides penalized.
- Send to a named person — a career-services staffer, chapter officer, or the instructor — not to `info@`. If the page has a suggestion form, use the form instead of email.
- One follow-up after 7 days, then stop. `outreach.mjs due` will tell you when.
